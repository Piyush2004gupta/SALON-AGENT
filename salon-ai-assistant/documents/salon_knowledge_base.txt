# Appointment Policy

Appointments can be booked online, through WhatsApp, or by phone.

Walk-ins are accepted subject to availability.

Customers should arrive at least 10 minutes before the scheduled appointment.

Late arrivals of more than 15 minutes may require rescheduling.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Appointments can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the appointment time.

Cancellations made within 24 hours may incur a cancellation fee.

No-show appointments may be charged 50% of the service fee.

---

# Refund Policy

Refund requests are accepted within 7 days.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are not available for completed services unless approved by management.

# Availability Rules

Each stylist can handle one appointment slot at a time.

Available slots are generated dynamically from the booking system.

If a slot is already booked, it is marked unavailable.

The chatbot should always check the availability tool before confirming an appointment.

---

# Booking Rules

The chatbot must never confirm a booking without checking availability.

The chatbot must collect:

* Customer Name
* Phone Number
* Branch
* Service
* Date
* Time

Only after successful availability verification should the booking be confirmed.
